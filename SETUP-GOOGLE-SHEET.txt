SETTING UP THE COMMON GOOGLE SHEET  (one-time, about 10 minutes)
Hypertension Screening app – Scudder College of Nursing

You need: a Google account (yours), a computer, and the file apps-script/Code.gs.
The sheet stays private to you. Phones can only ADD/UPDATE rows, and only if they
know your secret key.

A. CREATE THE SHEET AND PASTE THE SCRIPT
 1. Go to sheets.google.com and create a new blank spreadsheet.
    Name it, for example, "HTN Screening – All records".
 2. In the sheet, click the menu Extensions > Apps Script. A new tab opens.
 3. In the Apps Script tab, delete everything in the Code.gs box.
 4. Open apps-script/Code.gs (from the app folder or GitHub), copy ALL of it,
    and paste it into the empty Code.gs box.
 5. Near the top, find this line:
        const KEY = 'CHANGE-ME-TO-A-LONG-SECRET';
    Replace CHANGE-ME-TO-A-LONG-SECRET with your own secret key: at least
    20 letters and numbers, no spaces. Make it up yourself or use a password
    generator – do NOT reuse an example from anywhere. Keep the quote marks.
    Write the key down somewhere safe.
    (Do not change the PLACEHOLDER_KEY line below it.)
 6. Click the Save icon (floppy disk) or press Ctrl+S.

B. PUBLISH IT AS A WEB APP
 7. Click the blue Deploy button (top right) > New deployment.
 8. Click the gear icon next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
 9. Fill in:
        Description:      HTN sync
        Execute as:       Me (your email)
        Who has access:   Anyone
    Then click Deploy.
10. Google asks you to authorise. Click Authorize access, choose your account.
    If you see "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced >
    "Go to ... (unsafe)" > Allow. (This is normal for your own script.)
11. Copy the Web app URL shown (it starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/
    and ends with /exec). Click Done.
12. Optional check: paste that URL into a browser. You should see a short text
    starting with {"ok":true,"app":"htn-screening-sync" ... and "keyConfigured":true.
    If keyConfigured is false, you did not change the KEY in step 5 – fix it and
    see step 19.

C. CONNECT THE PHONES
13. On one phone, open the app: https://paulebe525.github.io/hypertension-screening/
14. Go to the Records tab > Sync settings.
15. Paste the Web App URL and type the secret key. Tap Save settings,
    then Test connection. You should see "Connected — key accepted ✓".
16. Easy way for everyone else: tap Copy setup link and send that link to your
    students/staff (WhatsApp or email). When they open it, the app asks
    "Set up Google Sheet sync on this phone?" – they tap OK and are done.
    The link contains the key, so share it only with your team.
17. From now on every save, edit and follow-up update is sent to the sheet
    automatically (tab "Records"). Without internet, records wait on the phone
    and are sent when the phone is back online, or when someone taps Sync now.
    The Records tab shows how many records are waiting, and each record shows
    "Synced" or "Not synced yet". Sample records are never sent.

D. GOOD TO KNOW
18. Do not rename the "Records" tab or delete the first (header) row. You may add
    your own extra columns at the right, or analyse the data in other tabs.
19. If you ever edit the script (for example to change the KEY): Deploy >
    Manage deployments > pencil icon > Version: New version > Deploy. The URL
    stays the same. If you change the KEY, every phone must enter the new key
    (or open a new setup link).
20. To stop all phones from sending: change the KEY (step 19), or
    Deploy > Manage deployments > Archive.
21. The sheet contains personal health data. Do not share the sheet itself
    publicly; give view access only to people on the research team.
